## Part A — Sourcing from China to Bangladesh (1688 process)

### The whole route, start to finish
1. Prepare: 1688 account, payment method, cargo agent with a Guangzhou warehouse.
2. Search products (photo search or Chinese keywords).
3. Shortlist 5–10 sellers.
4. Message all of them with the same opener.
5. Negotiate price, always for A grade.
6. Get real weight + master box data, calculate landed cost.
7. Bring samples from the best 2–3 sellers.
8. Get a written damage/QC agreement before paying.
9. Fix packaging (English / private label) and the shipping mark.
10. Place the order, have the seller adjust the price, write the remark, then pay.
11. Collect tracking number, packing list, invoice, carton photos.
12. Receive in Bangladesh (air about 7–12 days, sea about 40–50 days after Guangzhou), video the unboxing, check.
Key idea: a 1688 seller never ships to Bangladesh. His job ends at your agent's Guangzhou warehouse; the agent handles shipping, customs and delivery.

### Alibaba vs 1688
- 1688 = China's domestic wholesale market (Chinese language, prices in yuan, paid via Alipay). Alibaba = export market for foreign buyers.
- 1688 is cheaper because sellers price for local buyers, competition is huge, no export-staff / membership / trade-assurance overheads, and many factories sell directly. The same item is often half price or less on 1688.
- 1688: low MOQ (often 1–2 pieces), seller ships only inside China. Alibaba: English, can ship to Bangladesh, higher prices and MOQ.
- If buying on Alibaba: Verified Supplier + Trade Assurance, pay only inside the platform, 3–5+ years old, 90%+ response rate, check factory vs trader, ask shipping cost separately, and image-search the same item on 1688 to compare.

### Getting ready
- 1688 app + website; Chrome page translate and Google Lens make Chinese readable; chat with sellers in short English or translated Chinese.
- Paying: most Bangladeshis pay through an agent (pay in taka, agent pays in yuan at a slightly higher rate) or via their own Alipay. Rules: pay only through the 1688 order, never the seller's personal Alipay/WeChat; keep every receipt; check the agent's reviews first.
- Questions for a cargo agent: per-kg air rate and per-kg/CBM sea rate for your product type; is customs duty included; minimum weight; transit time; responsibility for loss/damage; Guangzhou warehouse address in Chinese + phone + your shipping mark.
- Agents usually quote an all-inclusive "door to door" per-kg rate; battery, liquid, cosmetics and clothing have different rates.

### Choosing sellers — 10 signals
Years on 1688 (3+ preferred) · factory or trader · repeat-buyer rate (回头率, above ~30% is good) · units sold · buyer photos in reviews · service scores (4.5+) · ships within 48 hours (stock exists) · strong-seller / big-factory badges · MOQ fits budget · tiered pricing. A price far below everyone else = warning (thin material, low weight, photo mismatch).

### Factory vs trading company
- Factory signs: words like 源头工厂 / 生产厂家 / 工厂直销 or 工厂/制品厂/实业 in the name; profile shows floor area, workers, machines, production line video; narrow product range; offers OEM/ODM; usually the oldest and cheapest of shops sharing the same photos; sends a factory video when asked.
- Trader signs: many unrelated categories, no machine photos, 贸易/商贸 in the name, avoids factory video.
- Start with a good trader for small test quantities; once a product sells, find the real factory — 1–2 yuan less per piece adds up fast.

### MOQ
- Shown as ≥2件 etc. (件/个 = piece, 套 = set, 双 = pair). Tiered prices show the saving from buying more (example: buy 100 instead of 90 if the price drops at 100).
- Use it to: check budget, spot factories (higher MOQ), negotiate ("small first order, bigger later"), and ask for lower MOQ on ready stock. Custom logo/packaging raises MOQ.
- Listed price may be for the smallest/lowest-grade version — confirm version, grade and quantity in chat.

### Products to avoid at the start
Batteries/power banks/liquids/sprays/gas · brand copies (customs and legal risk) · food, medicine, supplements, cosmetics · glass/ceramic/mirrors and other breakables · wireless devices that need BTRC approval · heavy-but-cheap items and bulky light items · clothes and shoes (size problems) · knives, air guns, shockers and other prohibited items.
Good starters: light, small, unbreakable, legal, needed in Bangladesh — phone accessories, kitchen gadgets, home organisers, car accessories, safe kids' toys, small fitness items.

### Talking to sellers
- Opener to every seller: bulk buyer, long-term, lowest price, A grade only.
- Then: ask the best price; confirm exactly the same product (photo); mention first order then growth; ask if stock ships today; ask free shipping to the Guangzhou warehouse.
- Save messages as Quick Replies; rename sellers with a Remark (product – factory – price – note).
- Keep everything in 1688 chat (it is your evidence). China is 2 hours ahead; message during their office hours (about 7am–4pm Bangladesh time).

### Negotiation and grades
- A grade = best quality (always ask for this), B = medium, C = low (avoid). Without stating the grade, sellers quote B/C prices.
- 7 tactics: collect 5–10 quotes and use them; talk about future orders; never reveal your budget; ask several things together (price, free domestic shipping, English packaging); use price tiers (100-piece price for 90 pieces); don't look in a hurry; get the final price confirmed in writing in chat.

### Weight, master box and landed cost
- Ask for a photo of one piece on a weighing scale — weight reveals quality (less weight = less material) and drives freight cost.
- Master box: pieces per box, gross weight, box size. Net weight = product only; gross = with packaging and carton (cargo charges gross).
- Air volumetric weight = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000 (some agents use 5000); you pay the higher of real and volumetric weight. Sea: by CBM or kg.
- Example (teaching numbers only): 300 phone holders at ¥8, 2 boxes of 16 kg → about ৳212 per piece by air vs about ৳164 by sea at the assumed rates. Sea is slower but cheaper per piece.
- Landed cost is not the final cost: add local packaging, delivery, ads, returns and damage. Rule of thumb: if you cannot sell at 2–3× landed cost, an ad-driven online product usually isn't profitable. If the numbers don't work, stop before ordering.

### Samples
- Always sample from the best 2–3 sellers before a bulk order; a few thousand taka on samples can save lakhs.
- Ask for SF Express (顺丰, 1–2 days inside China); get the waybill (运单号) number and give it to your agent.
- Hand Carry services bring small parcels fast (higher per-kg rate; no batteries/liquids).
- On arrival: video the unboxing, weigh it, use it for a few days, compare sellers side by side, ask potential buyers.

### QC and damage agreement
- Returning goods from Bangladesh to China is practically impossible, and goods arrive weeks later — so agree in writing before paying: wrong/damaged/defective pieces are replaced free in the next shipment (or refunded), 100% QC before packing. No agreement = don't pay that seller.
- After the sample: send the sample video and remind the seller the bulk must match it.
- Ask for photos/videos before packing; agents can open cartons in Guangzhou for a small fee.
- If problems arrive: video from the carton's shipping mark, count and photograph damaged units, send proof + the earlier agreement, ask for free replacement or partial refund, complain on 1688 if refused.

### Shipping mark, packaging, private label
- Shipping mark (given by the agent: code + your name/customer code, carton x/y, product, quantity) — without it cartons get lost in the warehouse.
- English or plain packaging sells better in Bangladesh than Chinese packaging.
- Private label (OEM): logo print, custom box, sticker/tag, custom colour. Higher MOQ (300/500/1000), work with factories, give a good logo file, approve a mockup or sample, allow 10–30 days, never use another brand.

### Placing the order
- Save the agent's Chinese address exactly; put your shipping mark in the receiver name and at the end of the address.
- Don't take the Chinese tax invoice (发票) — it adds tax you don't need; do ask for a simple invoice and packing list for customs.
- Submit the order but don't pay; ask the seller to change the price to the agreed one (改价), check the total, then pay.
- Remark in the order note and chat: green woven bags (编织袋), tracking number, packing list, simple invoice, carton photos with the shipping mark.

### After ordering
- Packing list: product, net weight per piece, pieces per box, box weight and size, number of boxes, total pieces, CBM, shipping mark — check the maths (boxes × pieces, CBM = L×W×H in metres).
- Send tracking, packing list, invoice and photos to the agent; consolidate multiple sellers into one shipment.
- Air for light/urgent/test quantities; sea for heavy or large lots once a product sells.
- Receiving: check the agent's bill and weight, inspect cartons, video the unboxing, count, compare with the sample, claim damage. Keep a record of every order.

### 10 common mistakes and scam signs
Mistakes: choosing the cheapest seller · bulk without samples · no landed cost calculation · no shipping mark · no damage agreement · paying the listed price instead of the negotiated one · not checking stock · putting all money into one product · agreements outside 1688 chat · starting with risky products.
Scam signs: asks for payment outside the platform · suspiciously low price · refuses weight photo or factory video · brand-new shop with no sales · good sample, bad bulk · short-shipped pieces. Agents too: hidden charges, inflated weight, delays — get rates in writing, check weight yourself, test with a small shipment.

## Part B — Finding winning products for Bangladesh

### Why "what everyone sells" loses (price war)
When 100 pages sell the same smart watch, prices race down while ad costs rise; after ad cost, delivery and 15–25% COD returns, profit per order shrinks to almost nothing or a loss. Big pages survive with bulk buying and large audiences. Customers have already seen the common products many times; a product they see for the first time makes them stop — that stop is the whole game. The easier road: a product few people in Bangladesh sell, that removes a specific pain, so there's no price comparison. Finding it takes about two weeks and saves years.

### What a winning product is — 7 checks (pass at least 5)
1. Removes a specific pain (health and pain-relief products are strongest — daily, intense).
2. Its job is obvious in a 10-second video.
3. Not easily found in local shops, Daraz or Facebook.
4. Sell at least 3× landed cost; sweet-spot selling price about ৳1,200–3,500.
5. Under ~500 g and smaller than a shoe box; avoid batteries, liquids, powders at first.
6. Doesn't break, doesn't spoil, one size fits all.
7. Allows repeat purchase or cross-sell (knee support → back belt → heat pad).
Health product ≠ medicine: focus on external-use items — supports, belts, cushions, massagers, heat pads, sleep, eye and foot comfort, baby safety, elderly care, kitchen safety.

### Where to find products
- **Instagram "account burn"**: a fresh account only for products; search hashtags (#amazonfinds, #tiktokmademebuyit, #painrelief, #kitchengadgets, #momhacks, #problemsolved …); watch problem-solving videos fully, save, share; skip everything else; 30–40 min a day, 3–4 days. Signals: views far above follower count, buying comments ("where to buy", "price?"), same product from 5–6 creators, video from the last month. Keep a sheet. Ask: does my neighbour have this problem?
- **Pinterest**: a visual search engine where people look for solutions; search problems, not product names; follow suggestions; check where pins lead and the price there. Pinterest Trends (no Bangladesh — watch the USA for the future and India for fit): rising lines, seasonal patterns, fast-growing related keywords.
- **TikTok**: burn a feed with product hashtags; TikTok Creative Center Top Products — watch USA, UK, Malaysia, Indonesia; last 7 days; health, beauty, household, sports; pick ranks ~10–40 with few ads but high click rate.
- **YouTube**: validate, don't discover — long reviews reveal weak points; search in Bangla to see if it's already in Bangladesh; Shorts audiences skew older and richer.
- **Meta Ad Library**: an ad running 2+ months with many copies is making money. Search Pakistan, India, Malaysia, Egypt (similar markets, 2–3 years ahead) with terms like pain relief, posture, knee, sleep, cash on delivery; check whether Bangladesh has it yet; learn the ad structure, don't copy. Trust a product only when 3+ sellers run long ads.
- **Marketplaces**: Amazon Movers & Shakers (Health & Household, Sports, Home & Kitchen, Baby, Beauty) and 1–2 star reviews for weaknesses to fix with suppliers; AliExpress order counts and buyer photos; Temu best sellers (same suppliers as 1688); Daraz to measure competition and read Bangladeshi complaints — fix the common complaint and lead your ad with it.
- **Google Trends (Bangladesh)**: no data = you must create demand; rising = move fast.
- **Problem-first list from daily Bangladeshi life**: back/neck/knee pain from desk jobs and buses; motorbike riders; mothers standing in kitchens; elderly parents; phone-tired eyes; poor sleep (heat, mosquitoes, noise, load-shedding); heat and power cuts; small busy kitchens (oil splatter, fish cutting); babies (parents spend most here, repeat buyers); prayer, mosque, Hajj/Umrah (seasonal); motorbike and ride-share needs. Search the problem on Amazon, Pinterest, then 1688 in Chinese.

### 6-step validation
1. Demand (works in 2+ places, and the problem exists here).
2. Competition in Bangladesh: 0–5 sellers green, 5–15 yellow, 15+ red.
3. Money: landed cost vs selling price at about 3×, include returns (delivery success is roughly 75–85%) and ad cost per sale.
4. Can it be imported easily (no liquids, powders, big batteries, magnets, sharp items; duty differences)?
5. Can you show problem → solution in a 15-second phone video?
6. Sample — never skip.

### After the sample
- Torture-test it for a week the way a customer would.
- Make your own videos at home (not the supplier's Chinese videos): first 3 seconds show the problem, then use, then result, then price/order; 15–30 seconds; make three with different openings.
- A simple website/landing page with COD order form sells pain-solving products better than Messenger.
- Ads: Facebook bills in dollars; many small sellers use agencies to pay in taka. Run real campaigns in Ads Manager, not Boost. Start around $3/day split across 3 creatives for 7 days, keep the winner; change product if nothing works in two weeks.
- Delivery: Pathao, Steadfast, RedX; call every customer to confirm — it cuts returns sharply; don't ship to people who don't answer.

### Mistakes to avoid
Falling in love with a product and skipping validation · launching many products at once · the cheapest seller · copying foreign ads · pricing too low (stay at 3×) · stopping research once something sells · trying to do everything alone (focus on product choice and understanding customers) · reading without acting.

### Bonus playbooks
- AI prompts: ask for 10 real pain points in everyday Bangla; ask for three 15–30 s scripts (3 s hook, 10 s demonstration, 5 s call to action).
- Upsell (2-piece bundle) and cross-sell (related small item, one delivery charge; tick-box add-on on the order form; offer at the end of the confirmation call).
- Confirmation call within minutes of the order (2 hours at most); read back the address; hold orders from hesitant customers.
- Facebook policy: no medical promises ("cures", "pain gone in 7 days"), no personal-attribute questions ("Do you have back pain?"), no before/after images, warm up new ad accounts with $2–3/day for the first week, keep account name/date of birth matching ID, request review from Account Quality if restricted.

Sourcing and factory selection · sample verification · shipping and customs handling · one piece or 500 pieces · send a product photo or 1688 link and get a cost estimate · website/landing page and ad support.
